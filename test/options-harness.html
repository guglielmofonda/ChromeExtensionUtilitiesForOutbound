    Keeping the account safe

    This tool only pre-fills the composer — it never sends, schedules, or scrapes. What X actually enforces is what and how much you send, even manually:

    • Duplicates are detected. Sending identical DMs (or the same link) to many accounts triggers a temporary DM block — X states this officially. Personalize beyond the first name and rotate templates.
    • Pace like a human. Stay well under ~30–50 templated first-contact DMs a day, ≤ 10–15 an hour. The hard platform cap is 500/day; getting anywhere near it is itself a spam signal.
    • No links in a first message. Share links after they reply.
    • If you hit a soft block, stop for the day. Don't retry.
    • Never automate the send. Auto-send, queueing, or bulk messaging is permanent-suspension territory. This extension will never do it.
