    Keeping the account safe

    This tool only pre-fills the open composer or connection note. It never sends, schedules, queues, or builds contact lists. You remain responsible for every message you review and send:

    • Make every message relevant. Personalize beyond a first name; repeated or unwanted outreach can trigger spam controls on either platform.
    • Use it at a human pace. If either service warns, limits, or restricts the account, stop instead of retrying.
    • Keep first contact low-pressure. Prefer warm context and avoid repetitive links or follow-up barrages.
    • LinkedIn is stricter about extensions. Its User Agreement restricts add-ons that scrape, automate, or modify the service; this tool minimizes automation but cannot eliminate policy risk.
    • Never automate the send. This extension has no auto-send, queue, bulk-message, or conversation-iteration path.
